05Sources and method

Where the data comes from.

Everything on this page is drawn from one query to the NASA Exoplanet Archive (Caltech/IPAC, operated for NASA under the Exoplanet Exploration Program). The query, the fetch date and the counts are recorded in data/meta.json, and the page’s captions are computed from the data at build time rather than typed.

Reading the page

The sky map uses equatorial coordinates (right ascension 0–360°, declination −90° to +90°). The system browser spaces orbits on a log scale of semi-major axis (estimated from period and stellar mass where the axis is not published) and compresses extreme period ratios so slow outer planets still move.

Planet classes in the system browser follow radius: below 1.6 R⊕ rocky, 1.6–4 sub-Neptune, 4–8 Neptune-like, above 8 giant. The habitable band on the insolation chart spans 0.32 to 1.55 times Earth’s flux, a common optimistic range.
